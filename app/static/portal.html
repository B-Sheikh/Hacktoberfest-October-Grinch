A FEW THINGS TO KNOW

Clear answers.
Before you report.

More clarity, less uncertainty.

Do I need an account or an SMS link?

No civilian account is needed. You can choose an office alert here, or use a personal reporting link received through the SMS workflow.

What if there is no alert for my area?

Use “Show all available alerts” to check other events. The office needs to create an alert before a report can be attached to that event.

What should I include in a report?

Your situation, the number of people affected, one to four photos, a location and any useful observations. Only describe what you can see from a secure position.

What happens to my photos?

Photos and notes are saved for office review. When live analysis is enabled, resized photos are also sent to Google for scene analysis. The report form explains the current processing mode.

Does submitting a report send emergency help?

No. Waterline does not contact emergency services. Your receipt confirms that a report was saved; it does not confirm a response or dispatch.
