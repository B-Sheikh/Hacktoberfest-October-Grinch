What happens next?

  1. Tell us whether you need help and how many people are affected.
  2. Add one to four photos and any observations.
  3. Pin where the photos were taken and send your report.

The office can review the evidence and coordinate responders. A receipt confirms that your report was saved; it does not confirm that a team has been dispatched.

An office alert is needed to attach your report to the correct event. If no matching alert is available, the office must create one.
